- Before the pick, park the left hand at pitch 75 yaw 0 with path='swing', about 3 cm from the
  sheet's centre toward the robot, 18 cm to its left and 18 cm above it: its wrist camera looks
  down on the whole sheet at 0.7-0.75 mm per pixel (probe L0, L5 r5, r6; probe L0, V1, L5 r9).
- The pen's top: a head pixel on the pen gave the pen with its holder, 8.6-8.8 x 2.5-2.9 x
  14.5-14.9 cm, top 4.5-4.9 mm low, every first time (L0, V1, L5, V6 r6, r8; 16/16); the grasp
  then sat that much low and the tip wrote nothing for 30 moves (L0 r6, two runs). By words or
  the Thing itself it came out 1.7 x 1.2-1.3 x 7.3-7.6 cm, top within 1 mm (L0, V1, L5, V6 r8 8/8).
- Pick (L0, V1, L5, V6 r8 8/8; probe L0, V1, L5 r9 6/6): side grasp, hand level, pitch 0 yaw 45,
  start 8 cm back along the fingers, straight in to 3.4 cm below the pen's top, close (0.7-0.8
  cm), lift 8.5 cm in one line move at pitch -30.
- Write with the hand level at yaw 55: yaw 35 and 45 were refused at the near points (L0 t4-t6
  r5). The tip is about 3.3 cm back toward the wrist and 10.7 cm below the grasp point; write
  2.5 mm above the surface (probe L0, L5 r5, r6, r9).
- Letter points from this view came out 0.4-1.3 mm off the band's middle, all the same way (probe
  L0, V1, L5 r6, r9); the tip mark, seen in the same view, takes that out with it.
- Tip check, an 8 mm line (6 moves): from the hover go straight down to the writing height, draw
  8 mm along the first stroke, lift straight up 1 cm, then up to 3 cm and 4 cm to the robot's
  right, and look; new offset = the line's middle minus the mean .at of its two moves; go back
  down at the first point. It showed 6.5-10 mm long and moved the offset 0.2-2.2 mm across itself
  (probe L0, V1, L5 r6, r9): the A passed 2/2 and the B 2/2 with it (probe L0, V1 r9).
- On the T the line lies along the bar, so the stem's side rests on where the line's ends read:
  the stem lay 1.4-1.6 mm beside its band once and the T passed 1 of 2 (probe L5 r9).
- So for the T draw an L: 6 mm along the bar, then 6 mm at a right angle away from the letter, in
  one pen-down path; lift and look as above. Slide the planned L over its ink and take the shift
  that lays both legs on it best (every 0.5 mm, then finer): new offset = the old one plus that
  shift. Fits came out 0.4 mm rms; the T passed 2/2 in 14 motions after the pick, the ink within
  0.45 mm of the band's middle (probe L5 r9).
- A line read as two dark streaks about 5 pixels apart: the wrong one was taken, the ink lay about
  4 mm beside the band and the A failed (V6 r8): take the streak on the stroke's band.
- Line moves of 10 mm took 5-6 steps and ended 0.3-0.6 mm off with no steady direction (probe
  L0, L5 r5, r6): aim back by the mean miss anyway (probe L0, L5 r6, r9).
- Along the paper speed=0.05. A and T in moves of up to 30 mm, 17-24 steps each: the A passed 2/2
  in 13-15 motions after the pick (probe L0 r9). The B as 15 moves, each straight piece one move of
  35-40 mm (stem halves, bars) and 5 chords of 13-15 mm per bowl: 2/2 in 16 (probe V1 r9).
- Without speed: 10-11 mm apart, one pass (probe L0, L5 r6).
- Do not press: lower touches moved the pen in the hand (probe L0, L5 r5). Lifts, waits, retraces
