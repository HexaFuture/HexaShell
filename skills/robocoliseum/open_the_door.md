# open_the_door

## Judging
- Stages in order: base in front of the door's middle, facing it; door swung open (turning the
  lever alone does not count); base inside the room, turned round; door shut from inside.
- All eight learning runs passed three stages; no close got below 57 degrees (28 once). Passed
  stages stay passed. tN = turn of the 2nd learning run; "1st run" marks the first.

## Flow
1. Measure 'door handle'; drive once: lever 0.7-0.8 m ahead, 0.15-0.3 m right (L0-L15; 7/8).
   With it 0.07 m right, level grasps were BLOCKED (L15 1st run).
2. Right hand level on the lever 3-6 cm left of its centre: pitch 0, yaw 0, roll 90, grip=1;
   close; press 6-8 cm down, 2-3 cm right, rolling to 40-55, contact=True (L0-L15; 7/8).
3. Keep it pressed; left fist (grip='close') on the door 0.25-0.35 m left of and 0.1 m above the
   lever, contact=True; open and draw back the right hand; push the fist 0.13-0.2 m on; drive
   0.3 m: 36-60 degrees open (L0-L15; 7/8). Still shut: press 8 cm down at roll 40 (L15 t12).
4. Hands tucked 0.35-0.5 m ahead, 0.2-0.27 m out, about 1 m up, pitch -15 to -30; drive straight
   in about 1 m; turn 180 (L0-L15; 8/8). The door is then wide open.
5. The open leaf lies along the wall by the hinge, lever face toward you; pushes there open it.
   The lever goes straight off the wall, then curves to the doorway; left alone it stays (L0-L15).
6. Lever 0.5-0.7 m ahead, 0.2-0.3 m right, 0.92-0.99 m up; level grip, pitch 0, roll 90
   (held 14 times; L0-L15).
7. Arm pull about 0.2 m back and 0.05-0.1 m left, yaw 15-20, contact=True: 90 to 70 (L10 t32),
   81 to 66 (L15 t52), 90 to 82 (L0 t27); no gain once (L5 t68).
8. Still holding, drive 0.2-0.3 m straight back: 9-14 degrees more though the base barely moved
   (L0 t33, L5 t66, L15 t69). Open fingers hooked round the free edge, driving back: 89 to 63 in
   three drives (L15 t17, t19).
9. Repeat: open, draw the hand 0.1 m straight back, drive back 0.2 m, regrip, pull (untested).
10. Once the leaf points at you, its room-side face faces you: push it near the free edge toward
   the doorway with a fist, following with the base until shut (untested).

## Common failures and fixes
- Waist turns holding at 77-90 degrees slipped within 1-3 turns (L0 t22, L5 t42, t54, t71, L15
  t45, t56), or left the door at 90 and twisted the base 43 degrees (L10 t16-t18). Only from 69,
  gripped from above at pitch 45, did they reach 28 (L15 1st run).
- Base turns while holding: the hand was pushed 24 cm off and slipped (L0 t38, L10 t19-t20).
- After a slip or release, home(), waist(yaw=0), a tuck or a drive with the hand by the leaf
  swung it back to 84-90 (L0 t23, t39; L5 t46, t55; L15 t35, t57, t76): open, draw back first.
- Drives holding the lever barely moved the base, once with "as asked" in the reply (L0 t33,
  L10 t33, L15 t53): judge by the head picture.
- measure('door handle') inside found nothing or a part (L0 t14, L5 t24): point at the lever.

## Final checks
- Before driving in: door open, right hand open, hands tucked (L0-L15).
- After a release: hand clear of the leaf before home(), waist or drive (L0, L5, L15).
