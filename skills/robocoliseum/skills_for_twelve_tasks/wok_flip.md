# wok_flip
## Judging
- In order: hold the pan up off the stove by its handle, then toss: one fast flick that throws the
  food up well clear of the rim, the pan still held up; then finish. Use the right hand throughout
  (V2: with the left hand both failed). Before the flick never tip the food out over the rim.
## Flow
1. Before any motion, pair= between the two wrist pictures on the top of the handle's tip and on
   the top of the dark bar's end at the pan; redo a pair that warns (L0, L1 r1-r3; V1, V2 and r3).
2. Use the right hand: hand('right', 'wrap', 1), then 18 cm straight above the grasp point, level
   and pointing left across the bar (pitch 0, yaw 90, roll -90), one line (L0-V2 r3; probe L0, L1).
3. The grasp point: 6.5 cm from the tip along the bar toward the pan, 2.6 cm below the bar's top
   there (top height from the two pairs). Straight down to it, then gripper('right', 'close')
   (L0-V2 r3, 6/6; probe L0, L1; 11/11 held through the toss; 6 cm, 2.5 cm also held: probe L0).
4. Lift in two moves, without grip=: by=(0, 0, 0.04), wait 10 steps; by=(0, 0, 0.08), wait 36
   steps (0.6 s). The pan comes up about 17 cm, far side 11-13 degrees up (L0-V2 r3; probe L0, L1).
5. Then down 3 cm turning to roll -75 (the far side down; the pan then tilted 6-7 degrees), wait 20
   steps; keep the arm otherwise where the lift left it (L0-V2 r3, 6/6; probe L0, L1; 10/10 held).
6. The toss, one goto: by=(0, 0, 0.27), roll=-110: 27 cm straight up in 26-27 steps, stopping hard,
   the far side turning up to 36-38 degrees; the rim reached 3.1-3.3 m/s, the food flew 40-60 cm
   over the rim (L0-V2 r3, 6/6; probe L0, L1; 7/7).
7. Then call finish at once, the pan still in the hand, whatever the pictures show: do not wait,
   move down, flick again or wait for a report of success first (probe L0, L1; 4/4).
## Verified parameters
- The handle: a dark round bar on the pan's near side; Flow 1's pairs give both its ends (L0, L1
  r1-r3; V1, V2 and r3).
## Common failures and fixes
- 4.5 cm behind the tip the pan fell out on the lift (probe L0; 0/2); 5.5 cm behind, any move down
  after the lift let it swing onto the stove (probe L0; 3/3 lost). Use Flow 3's point.
- Ending at roll -120 the pan stood 46-48 degrees far side up and the food slid out (probe L0, L1;
  0/2); -90 to -110 and -75 to -105 tossed 1 of 3 (probe L0, L1). Use -75 to -110.
- Slower flicks (rim 1.2-1.6 m/s: roll 65 to 110 with the left hand, V2's moves with the right)
  left the food 6-10 cm above the rim (V2 t9-t11; probe L1; 0/2). Use one fast straight goto.
- Waits and two more flicks after the first (L0, V2 r3; 3/3) threw only the little food still in
  the pan; their turns to roll -65 or -70 let the pan sink to 4-8 cm over the stove, once onto it
  (V2 r3). Finish after the first flick (Flow 7).
- After the lift a move 6 cm forward and 7 cm up turned the pan down in the hand (V1 t7); grip= on
  a move after the close dropped the pan, and a tipped pan was not grasped again (L0, L1 r2; 0/3).
## Final checks
- Before closing: the bar under the palm between the open fingers; after the lift: the pan up off
  the stove, the food in it; head pictures (L1 r2 t6, V1 t3, t5; L0-V2 r3).
- After the flick the food flies, then mostly lies out of the pan, over its near rim (L0-V2 r3;
  6/6): finish anyway, without a wait or a second flick.
