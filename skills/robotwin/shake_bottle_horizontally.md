# shake_bottle_horizontally

## Judging
- The bottle meant lies on its side left or right of the table's middle; bottles standing
  upright elsewhere are not it.
- Done the moment the held bottle comes up off the table: every success ended 1-2 steps into the
  lift after the close, before any sideways shake ran. A lift that does not end it holds the
  wrong thing or nothing.
- Use the arm the instruction names; it was always the one on the bottle's side.

## Flow
1. Measure it, take the wrist look-down pose the measure suggests, measure again (10 eps).
2. Pitch 90, yaw = grip_yaw, open, over its middle; straight down until the grasp point is 3-5 cm
   above the table top; close; if holding, lift 10-20 cm in the same cell, then shake (10 eps).

## Verified parameters
- Grasp point 2.7-5.8 cm above the table top: closed on 6.4-8.2 cm and held (10 eps). Take that
  height from the card's table top: the measured centre read 1-3 cm high (6 eps).

## Common failures and fixes
- Gripped an upright bottle standing elsewhere and shook it sideways: nothing ended; about 440
  steps and 13 replies went before the lying one was taken (1 ep). A measure by name found
  another object first in 2 more (2 eps).
- Grasp point 5.9 cm above the table, at the measured centre's height: slipped out on the lift,
  the bottle rolled 12 cm; about 90 steps and 3 replies to retake it (1 ep).

## Typical step costs
- Limit 700. 39-44 steps in 3-8 replies: look-down 16-25, descend and close 16-19 (8 eps).
