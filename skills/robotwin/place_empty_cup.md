# place_empty_cup

## Judging
- Done when the cup stands upright on the coaster, centred on it, resting on it rather than held
  above it, released: about 4 cm off its centre, at its edge, did not count (1 ep).
- Not judged: which arm, which way the cup is turned, the path.
- Not done: the cup at the coaster's edge or beside it, tipped over, or still held up.

## Flow
1. Measure the cup and the coaster, then measure each again from a wrist camera as the measure
   suggests (10 eps). From above, the cup's measure often says it rests on something 3-6 cm up
   (9 eps): ignore that, it stands on the table, its top 8.5-9.5 cm above it.
2. Use the arm on the cup's side; the coaster is near the middle (10 eps).
3. Grip from above at pitch 90, grasp point 1.5-4 cm below the cup's top (10 eps). The rim is
   nearly as wide as the open fingers: a grip aimed at the centre closed across the body, about
   8 cm, in 5 of 9 tries and caught one wall, under 1 cm, in 3 (8 eps). A wall grip on purpose,
   grasp point 2.5-3.6 cm from the centre along the closing line, also held (2 eps).
4. Lift 10-15 cm (8 eps). After a wall grip the cup's centre was 2.5-4.2 cm from the grasp point
   along the closing line, under 1 cm across it (3 eps). To learn which side, measure the held
   cup or point at it in the head picture (2 eps).
5. Carry high over the coaster's centre, shifted by that offset along the closing line only
   (4 eps). Lower the grasp point to 1.3-1.8 cm above the height it gripped at and open (7 eps).
   Success came 1-2 steps into the open (9 eps).

## Verified parameters
- The coaster measured 10.4 cm wide and 0.5-0.9 cm thick from a wrist camera (9 eps).
- Grips across the body closed at 7.8-8.1 cm, 2.7-3.9 cm below the measured top (5 eps). Wall
  grips closed at 0.6-0.8 cm and carried the cup from where it stood (5 eps).
- A release 3.7 cm above the gripping height also counted (1 ep).

## Common failures and fixes
- After a wall grip, a point on the held cup in the head picture put its centre 3 cm to the side
  of the closing line. The release followed it and the cup stood about 4 cm in front of the
  coaster's centre, at its edge: not counted (1 ep). Shift along the closing line only (4 eps).
- Gripping that cup's wall again where it stood, it slipped out during the lift; then the cup
  and the coaster vanished from every camera and were never found (1 ep).
- A first grip at a pointed spot closed on nothing and the cup ended up about 8 cm away;
  measured again from above, it was gripped across the body and placed (1 ep).

## Typical step costs
- Limit 500 steps and 40 replies. Successes took 65-260 steps in 4-14 replies, most 65-105 in
  4-8 (9 eps); the failure gave up at 324 steps after 17 replies (1 ep).
- Measuring 2-4 replies (26-64 steps); grip and lift 1-2 replies (17-38 steps); carry, lower and
  open 1-4 replies (15-22 steps) (8 eps).
