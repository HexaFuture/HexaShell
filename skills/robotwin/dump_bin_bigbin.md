# dump_bin_bigbin

## Judging
- Every wording ("pour into bin", "pour its contents away", "pour carefully", "empty it") means: all
  5 red balls (1.6 cm) from the small table bin into the big bag-lined bin on the floor past the
  table's left edge, while the small bin is still held up high over it (Flow 5). Hold it tipped
  there until success: set down or lowered first, or with a ball on the table, it may fail. Which
  arm picks it up does not matter.

## Flow
1. Priors: an open cup-like bin 11-15 cm tall, ~9 cm across, round or square; a few ~15 cm (20 eps).
2. Point at it in the head picture (18 eps; its name found the big bin or nothing, 7 eps), then look
   down on it with the wrist camera the hint names: its "top" was right (12 eps).
3. Only the left reached over the big bin (2 eps). Bin right of the middle: the left reached across
   level, yaw -30 to -40, from 10-13 cm back along that heading, 5-7 cm above the table; close
   7.4-7.9 cm (4 eps, all poured); from above it took a square mouth's body (1 ep) or a wide bin's
   wall nearest the middle (1 ep). Or the right grasps it, sets it down near the middle on the start
   line, opens, homes, and the left grasps it there (4 eps).
4. From above, pitch 90. Body grip, square mouths only: yaw = grip_yaw, over its centre, 4-5 cm
   below its top; close 7.7-8.1 cm (4 eps). Over a round mouth's centre the open fingers landed on
   its rim and knocked the cup over or off the table 3 times, took a wall by chance twice (5 eps):
   take a round one level (Flow 3) or by the rim. Rim grip: the wall between the fingers, 1.5-2 cm
   below the top (7 eps) or ~4 (2 eps); close 0.8-1.4 cm. Lift 12-20 cm straight up (15 eps).
5. Left: carry to 9-23 cm left of its start, 9-19 cm beyond it, 5-13 cm above the start height, at
   pitch 60-85 (90 was refused or short there, 6 eps), or level if gripped level (16 eps).
6. Turn only there. Gripped from above: pitch 0, then -20 to -30: success 2-19 steps after it began
   (10 eps); pitch 0 alone: nothing in 15-22 steps (5 eps), once success in 9 (1 ep); -45 refused (5
   eps). Gripped level: roll -130 to -140, top tipping left: success in 15-18 steps (4 eps).

## Verified parameters
- Held: level grips 4 of 4 (4 eps), square-mouth body grips 5 of 5 (4 eps), rim grips 10 of 12 (9
  eps; one ~1 cm outside the wall slipped on the lift). measure() put the big bin's centre straight
  beyond the left hand's start, seeing only its right part (18 eps).

## Common failures and fixes
- A dropped or knocked bin lost the episode (4 eps): balls spilled, or it left the table; spilled
  balls were not picked up or pushed in (1 ep), but once all had rolled into the big bin, and
  carrying the emptied bin over it ended it (1 ep). "Closed fully" after a lift: it slipped (2 eps).
  Turning a rim grip level while moving 7 cm at once dropped it (1 ep).
- Looking down into the open bin, measure() read a 2-7 cm slab on something 4-12 cm above the table
  (16 eps): it stands on the table; use its "top".

## Typical step costs
- Budget 600 steps, 40 replies. Clean runs 94-237 steps in 7-15 replies (14 eps): look-down 20-45,
  grasp and lift 18-37, hand-over 65-71 (3 eps), carry 17-32, turn and tip 9-41.
