# place_container_plate

## Judging
- The container is the bowl or cup the instruction names, even when the wording makes it sound
  like a tool ("use the X to place the container"); nothing else on the table needs moving.
- Done when it stands upright on the plate near the plate's centre, resting on the plate rather
  than held above it, released: success came one step into the open (8 eps).
- Not judged: which arm, which way the container faces, the path.
- Not done: held above the plate, tipped over or upside down, at the plate's edge.

## Flow
1. Measure the named container and the plate, then look down with a wrist camera as the measure
   suggests and measure again (10 eps). If the words find nothing or something else, or a
   measured size is far off, point at the object in the head picture (5 eps).
2. Use the arm on the container's side; the plate is near the middle on that side (10 eps).
3. A bowl: pinch its rim from above at pitch 90 (yaw 0 on a side rim, yaw 90 on the near or far
   rim) (6 eps). A cup: close across its body from above at pitch 90, 1.5-3.5 cm below its top
   (4 eps). Lift 10-13 cm and look before carrying (8 eps).
4. Carry high over the plate, with the grasp point as far from the plate's centre as it was from
   the container's centre: 4-5.5 cm for a rim pinch, none for a cup (8 eps).
5. Lower until the grasp point is 1.5-2.6 cm above the height it gripped at, so the bottom is
   about level with the plate's rim, and open. Success came one step into the open (8 eps).

## Verified parameters
- The plate is about 19 cm wide, its rim top 1.5-2.5 cm above the table (8 eps). Bowls were
  11-14 cm wide and 4.5-5.5 cm tall; cups 7-8 cm wide and 8-11 cm tall (10 eps).
- Rim pinches closed to 0.8-1.3 cm, often reading 0.7-0.9 cm after the lift with a note that the
  bowl shifted; it was carried fine (5 eps). Cup grips closed to 6.7-7.5 cm (4 eps).
- Pinches with the grasp point 1-2.7 cm below the bowl's measured top held (5 eps); two at about
  1 cm below slipped (2 eps), so about 2 cm below is the safer middle.

## Common failures and fixes
- A rim pinch slipped out during the lift, the gripper closing fully (2 eps). Once the bowl fell
  back and a second pinch about 2.7 cm below its top held (1 ep). Once the bowl flew off the
  table, knocking other objects over, and was never found again (1 ep).
- Once a cup stood upright on the plate about 1 cm from its centre, both hands open, and success
  never came; a second set-down, then moving other objects onto the plate, did not change it
  (1 ep). The named container on the plate is the whole task: do not move other objects.

## Typical step costs
- Limit 400 steps and 40 replies. Successes took 68-177 steps in 4-9 replies (8 eps); the
  failures stopped at 209 steps (gave up after 13 replies) and at 400 steps (21 replies) (2 eps).
- Measuring 2-7 replies (35-113 steps); grip and lift 1 reply (16-45 steps); carry, lower and
  open 1 reply (15-26 steps) (8 eps).
