# stack_bowls_two

## Judging
- Done when the bowls sit nested, one centred in the other, the lower one on the table, released;
  not when one rests on the other's rim, tilts or lies beside it. Not judged: which
  bowl is below (they are alike), where, which arm, nor setting the first one down as worded.

## Flow
1. Point at each bowl in the head picture (7 eps) or find them; measure each again from a wrist
   camera (10 eps). Each is about 13 cm wide and 5 cm tall.
2. Stack one onto the other where it stands only if one arm reaches pitch 90 about 10 cm above
   the lower bowl's top, over its matching rim spot, with no tilt note (4 eps). Where pitch 90
   was refused there, a bowl lowered onto it on a slant, tilted 4 degrees, and let go: both bowls
   left the view (1 ep).
3. Else set one down in the middle, its centre 2-11 cm farther from the robot than the hands'
   start grasp points, opening 0.6-1.6 cm above its gripping height, and measure it again from a
   wrist camera before bringing the other (14 eps).
4. Pinch a rim at pitch 90 closing across the wall (yaw 0 on a side rim, yaw 90 on the near rim),
   grasp point about 5.5 cm from the centre and 1 cm below the top; lift 8-14 cm (18 eps).
5. Lower the top bowl's grasp point over the base's centre plus the same offset to 2.5-5.5 cm
   above the base's top and open; success came one step into the open (18 eps).

## Verified parameters
- Pinches closed to 1.0-1.4 cm; the note that the bowl shifted after the lift was harmless (18 eps).

## Common failures and fixes
- With a rim pinch, the note that the gripper closed fully on the way meant the bowl had fallen
  (2 eps), and the hand was still opened at the target. Once the fallen bowl, taken for the other
  one, was let go near that empty target, partly over the other bowl: both left the view (1 ep).
- With a bowl, an arm 11 cm or more past the middle was refused (3 eps) or tilted 6 degrees, and
  the bowl slipped out on the way (1 ep). Set the base down in the middle instead (14 eps).

## Typical step costs
- Limit 900 steps. Successes with a base 181-360 steps in 6-15 replies (14 eps); in place 70-96
  in 7-11 (3 eps), 204 in 9 after a fall (1 ep). The failures gave up at 193 and 326 (2 eps).
