# move_pillbottle_pad

## Judging
- Done when the bottle stands upright on the flat blue square pad (about 8 cm a side, paper-thin)
  near its centre, released; lying down, tilted on the pad's edge or still held: not done.
- The bottle: an upright pill bottle 5.5-7.5 cm wide, 13-15.5 cm tall, named by colours, cap or
  label; other bottles may stand near. The pad is on its side of the table; use that arm.

## Flow
1. Measure both again from above: one-view pad centres were up to 3 cm off (20 eps); words caught a
   wrong object for the bottle (6 eps): check it is that cylinder. Keep the open hand higher than
   the bottle's top: at the pad's warning pose, 12-14 cm above the table, the fingertips came down
   on the bottle standing beside the pad and knocked it over (1 ep); from 19-25 cm the pad read as
   well (11 eps).
2. Top-down: pitch 90, yaw 0, from over its centre straight down to a grasp point 2-5 cm below its
   measured top, close (6.1-7.7 cm), lift 10-14 cm (11 eps). From the front at pitch 0-60, in line
   with its centre, same height, also held (3 eps). Where pitch 90 was out of reach (3 eps), pitch
   60 straight down from over its centre held (2 eps).
3. Carry over the pad's measured centre at the same angles, lower to 0.7-1.3 cm above the grasp
   height, open. Success came with the open (17 eps).

## Verified parameters
- Releases 0-0.3 cm (15 eps), 0.6-1.3 cm (3 eps) and 2 cm (1 ep, a bottle stood up in the hand) from
  the pad's measured centre all counted.
- Every grasp of a standing bottle that held began its last straight move less than 1 cm to the left
  or right of its measured centre (17 eps).

## Common failures and fixes
- Straight down from its measuring pose, 3 cm to the side of a 7 cm bottle, the fingertips came to
  rest on its shoulder (BLOCKED); in line with it from the front at pitch 30 it held (1 ep).
- Straight down over a spot picked on the cap in two pictures, 2.3 cm to the side of the (rough)
  measured centre: the bottle fell over, the close was empty (1 ep).
- Straight from where the pad was measured (10 cm to the bottle's side, below its top) to the grasp
  point at pitch 60: the hand swept the bottle over and 18 cm away; it was never held again in the
  350 steps left (1 ep). Go over its centre, or in line in front of it, first.
- A fallen bottle within reach was stood up (2 eps): from above across its body (pitch 90, yaw =
  grip_yaw, grasp point about 4 cm above the table; held 7.4-7.5 cm), lift to 21-25 cm above the
  table, turn to pitch 0 at the same yaw. The end the hand then points to rises; in both the cap was
  that end (cap at the other end: untested). Carry at pitch 0 over the pad, lower until the grasp
  point is 8-9 cm above the table, open: success (2 eps).

## Typical step costs
- Limit 400. 56-107 steps, 4-9 replies (17 eps): looks from above 17-39 (43-71 after a wrong
  bottle), grasp and lift 16-31 (43 at pitch 0), carry, lower and open 12-20. Knocked over and stood
  up: 107-221 steps, 10-18 replies; standing it up and placing took 63-65 (2 eps).
