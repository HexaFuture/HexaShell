# stack_bowls_three

## Judging
- Done when all three bowls sit nested in one stack, each centred inside the one below,
  anywhere on the table, released: success came one step into the last open (17 eps).
- Not judged: which bowl is at the bottom, the order, the stack's place, which arm moves which
  bowl. The three bowls are alike, so a size order or a named bottom bowl needs no sorting.
- Not done: a bowl resting on a rim, tilted, upside down, or beside the stack.

## Flow
1. Find the bowls: each about 12-13 cm wide. A search for "bowl" can also return other round
   things, so keep the three of that width (10 eps).
2. Build the stack in the middle front, where both arms reach: carry one bowl there and set it on
   the table (12 eps); or stack on a bowl at one side and pass the far bowl through the middle -
   set it down there with one hand, pick it up with the other (3 eps).
3. Pinch a rim wall from above at pitch 90, grasp point about 1 cm below the top of a measure
   reading the bowl 12-14 cm wide and 4-6 cm tall (11 eps), closing across the wall (yaw 90 on
   the near rim, yaw 0 on a side rim); lift 8-16 cm (17 eps).
4. Carry it high, then lower the same grasp point over the matching rim spot of the stack (stack
   centre plus the offset the grasp had from the carried bowl's centre) and open (17 eps).

## Verified parameters
- A rim pinch closes to 0.8-1.5 cm (20 eps); after the lift it often reads 0.7-1.0 cm with a note
  that it shifted in the fingers: 3 of 40 such pinches then lost the bowl on the way (17 eps).
- Grasp point from 1 cm above to 0.5 cm below the top of a 12-14 cm wide measure: 11 of 39 pinches
  lost the bowl on the way or caught nothing (13 eps); 0.7-2 cm below, none of 24 did (11 eps).
- Release, grasp point above the bottom bowl's measured top: second bowl 3-6 cm, third 5-8 cm
  (17 eps). A bowl set on the table: open 0.3-1.5 cm above where it was gripped (15 eps).
- With a bowl at pitch 90, an arm reached 7 cm past the middle (1 ep); the right arm 12 cm past it
  tilted the bowl 4-6 degrees and could not lift after the release (1 ep); at 18 cm, refused (1 ep).

## Common failures and fixes
- An open finger came down on the stack: the hand pinched a bowl's near rim with the stack 2-3
  cm beyond it, and the stack tipped apart (1 ep). The open fingers reach about 4.5 cm each side
  of the grasp point along the closing line: pinch the side of the rim away from other bowls.
- With a rim pinch, the note that the gripper closed fully on the way meant the bowl had fallen,
  each time (8 eps): one onto the stack, which came apart (1 ep); two off the front edge (1 ep).
- Never held again in about 15 grips from above and the side: a fallen bowl upside down near the
  front edge, and one let go 4 cm above the base, as usual, that landed tilted against it (2 eps).

## Typical step costs
- Limit 1200 steps and 40 replies. Successes took 216-896 steps in 9-35 replies (17 eps); the
  failures stopped at 478 and 686 steps (gave up) and at 1200 steps (40 replies) (3 eps).
- Finding and measuring 3-7 replies (0-70 steps); per bowl, pinch and lift 1 reply (15-45
  steps), then carry, lower, open and go home 1 reply (15-55 steps) (8 eps).
