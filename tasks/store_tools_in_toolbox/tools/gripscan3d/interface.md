`robo gripscan3d --rect '[x0,y0,x1,y1]' [--seed '[u,v]'] [--expand_pixels 32] [--angle A] [--support_z Z] [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--min_width .012] [--max_width .065] [--span .012] [--finger_clearance .006] [--opening_width W]` surveys calibrated depth without motion or action steps.
Seed selects the depth-connected raised component containing that exact integer pixel inside rect; missing/background/undersampled seeds fail without snapping. Without seed, multiple qualifying components fail with pixel bounds and representative surface pixels. Returns seed, selected_component and selection_source; identity is geometric, not semantic. Neighboring geometry remains in the obstacle survey.
Rect is an inclusive pixel rectangle; A is horizontal finger-opening direction in degrees; omitted A derives the perpendicular to each connected silhouette’s area-weighted principal axis. Ambiguous derived axes fail; either angle mode requires a complete silhouette. A seeded clipped silhouette permits one expansion by expand_pixels (integer 0–128 per edge, default 32, zero disables), then clipping still fails. Unseeded scans do not expand; expansion preserves a supplied angle. Returns requested_rect, evaluated_rect, crop_expanded and initial_crop_diagnostics after expansion. Widths/span are meters: .002 <= min_width < max_width <= .15, span .006–.04, finger_clearance 0–.03 (zero disables).
Omitted support_z estimates a broad horizontal border band, with up to two surrounding-crop searches (proportional padding, then 32 pixels per edge) on the same frame, without widening candidate selection; candidate bounds use evaluated_rect; expansion preserves the exact seed and original support elevation. Ambiguous support or inconsistent supplied elevation fails with diagnostics.
Returns up to five candidates: center_xy, surface_z, contact_z_estimate, height_above_support, width, width_range, angle, angle_source, axis_variance_ratio, pixel, component, samples and height_samples; height uses the central 60% of each longitudinal third.
Contact Z is the support-to-top midpoint for a resting body, with no calibrated TCP offset. Returns support_search_rects and effective_span (expanded for pixel spacing, at most .04 m), support_source and support_rect.
Ranks by balance_offset_fraction from a visible column-volume centroid, then height and width; insufficient triangle evidence falls back to length midpoint. Returns balance_reference, visible_balance_xy, balance_offset_m/fraction, longitudinal_offset_m/fraction and visible_longitudinal_span_m; longitudinal fields always refer to length midpoint. Hidden shape, density and crop truncation can bias ranking; mass/balance are unverified.
Rejects narrow, split, uneven, truncated or undersampled sections. Three raised depth samples in either lateral finger-clearance band veto a section, including neighbors outside rect; returns side_obstacle_samples and finger_clearance. Optional opening_width W (.002–.20 m) is the physical open aperture: sections exceeding it fail; lateral surveys extend from the silhouette through the open finger positions plus finger_clearance, even with zero clearance. Returns opening_width and per-candidate side_sweep_extents_m. Omission retains the narrow edge survey. Missing depth, inclined approach, finger height and hidden surfaces are unverified; collision_checked=false, reachability and retention unverified.
Returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason; invalid arguments, missing depth or no qualifying section fail. No-section diagnostics include support provenance, effective_span, raised_samples, component_count, span/width/height-variation ranges and rejection_counts; null ranges mean unobserved, counts describe rejection stages rather than accepted candidates.
