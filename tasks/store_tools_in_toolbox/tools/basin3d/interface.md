`robo basin3d --rect '[x0,y0,x1,y1]' --seed '[u,v]' --rim_z Z [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--expand_pixels 32]` reads calibrated depth without motion or action steps.
Rect is inclusive; seed is an exact integer pixel in a connected depression 4–80 mm below the caller-observed horizontal rim elevation Z in world meters. No snapping.
Clipping retries once on the same observation with expand_pixels (integer 0–128) extra pixels per edge, retaining seed and rim; 0 disables expansion. Reports requested_rect, evaluated_rect, crop_expanded and initial_plan_detail on retry, including failure diagnostics.
Returns center_xy (visible floor area centroid), floor_z, rim_z, depression_depth_m, floor_area_m2, principal_extents_m, samples, floor_samples and reference_kind.
Multiple levels, disconnected floor regions, or a seed outside the dominant floor fall back to the exact seed's connected ±3 mm horizontal floor, enclosed by evidence at least 4 mm higher. A 3–4 mm transition band may extend at most 6 mm along calibrated XY paths; missing/lower evidence or an unbounded band fails. floor_segmentation identifies uniform_depression or seeded_enclosed_floor; wall_transition_samples counts traversed band pixels.
axis_angle_deg is the horizontal major-axis angle in world degrees, modulo 180; axis_variance_ratio below 1.5 returns null and axis_ambiguous=true. End direction is always ambiguous; extents follow principal axes, including when angle is null.
Returns plan_ok/plan_fail_reason; invalid inputs, missing boundary depth, clipped regions, inconsistent rim evidence, uneven floors or insufficient horizontal area fail.
Enclosure failures include diagnostics.floor_failure and available seed_z/seed_horizontal, horizontal counts/fraction and candidate_floor_z; boundary failures add failure_pixel in full-image coordinates, boundary_z/point_world (null for missing depth) and boundary_path_m when available. geometry_valid=false: these are rejection evidence, not accepted geometry. Crop-expansion provenance is retained.
Center and elevation describe visible geometry, not a TCP pose or corresponding physical landmark. No contact offset is applied; fit, occlusion, hidden shape and seating remain unverified.
