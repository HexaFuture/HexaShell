`robo round_center --u U --v V [--camera head|wrist_l|wrist_r] [--pixels 8]`: motion-free convex depth fit with bounded radial refinement; returns world center, radius, RMS, samples and normalized cap support; rejects invalid/nonconvex fits or RMS >1 mm.
`robo release_clearance --x X --y Y --z Z [--radius 0.05]`: motion-free head-depth column check; returns coverage, ceiling with its head pixel/world point, bounded release-height status and spatially separated candidates; rejects missing depth, obstructions or required height correction >30 mm. Candidates pass the same release-height check and retain Z; no support claim. Radius range .03–.10 m.
`robo checked_pick ARM --x X --y Y --z Z [--open x|y] [--aperture 0.75] [--offset 0] [--clearance 0.09] [--lift 0.09]`: downward grasp and vertical lift; clearance/lift .04–.20 m, offset -.01–.03 m; local depth/load extent bounds approach and exit height.
`robo checked_transfer ARM --x X --y Y --z Z --to_x X --to_y Y --to_z Z [--open x|y] [--aperture 0.75] [--offset 0] [--margin 0.05] [--radius 0.05] [--segment 0.08]`: grasp, lift, depth-covered transit, vertical release, opening and retreat; requires commanded open gripper.
ARM=left|right; positions/lengths are absolute world coordinates/metres; source XYZ is a center and destination XYZ a nominal TCP release point. Open defaults to x and offset to 0 (fitted center); aperture .5–1; transfer offset -.01–0, margin .03–.12, radius .03–.10, segment .03–.20, with loaded horizontal portions capped at .08.
Fits compare central and off-axis broad caps; support <.25 triggers bounded peripheral search before selecting the best accepted fit within unchanged center/radius/RMS gates. Both grasp commands require a fresh source fit within 12 mm, preshape overhead and fully close; missing source or exclusive positive lift evidence fails. A qualified near-TCP depth obstruction permits at most one open-arm view relocation and fresh checks; near-centered views select an IK-reachable lateral TCP-axis direction; no grasp retries or rollback.
Transfer checks the release column, plans covered capsules with bounded detours and expands obstacle clearance for observed load extent while preserving the requested endpoint margin. Optional interior detours lower peaks ≥20 mm without raising terminal height or total geometric travel; persistent view obstructions require a route ≤40 mm above the pre-refresh TCP. Excessive height, missing depth, out-of-bounds calibration or envelope fails; release Z is max(depth floor, observed-load envelope floor, grasp calibration), raised at most 30 mm above request; XY stays fixed. The observed-load floor uses the expanded radius and vertical allowance; missing depth or correction >30 mm stops before transport.
Read-only IK chain checks before grasp and after lift reject unreachable routes; estimates are not collision or retention evidence. Loaded horizontal portions are ≤80 mm, each ending in a retention check; every height change precedes horizontal travel; downward transport and release portions are ≤30 mm. Retention is checked after each loaded segment and before opening using a fixed post-lift anchor; absent/ambiguous evidence returns transport_not_visually_confirmed; upward center drift >8 mm from that anchor during commanded lowering or unexpected measured drops >8 mm returns descent_load_tracking_failed before further motion or opening.
Motion stops on planning failure, clipping, episode end or TCP error >8 mm / 6 degrees; grasp-only shallow contact allows ≤15 mm total/upward and ≤10 mm XY error under source-height checks and mandatory lift evidence. Every motion costs action steps.
Returns plan_ok, plan_fail_reason, stages, release_commanded, retention and optional localization/route/pick_exit diagnostics; grasp_verified:false and placement_verified:false. Failure after opening can retain release_commanded:true.
Depth and surface matching cannot establish identity, seating, vacancy, unseen clearance or reachability; candidates never execute automatically. checked_pick covers only vertical exit; checked_transfer also guards loaded transit and descent.
