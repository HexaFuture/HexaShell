`robo recess_target --u U --v V --load_radius R [--model recess|surface] [--pixels 8] [--tcp_offset 0] [--gap 0.005] [--radius 0.03]`
Motion-free head-depth measurement at pixel U,V; default recess fits a smooth bowl or bounded planar floor. Surface samples a continuous 5×5 patch without fitting a center or asserting concavity; no action cost.
R is vertical load bounding radius .01–.05 m; tcp_offset is TCP Z minus load-center Z, -.03–.03 m; pixels 3–24 (recess mode only); gap .003–.03 m; column radius .03–.10 m plus 10 mm allowance.
Recess mode returns world bottom, release_tcp, surface ceiling, residual, curvature, geometry model, samples, column diagnostics, plan_ok and plan_fail_reason. A bounded planar center may be ≤R from the selected world point; it requires nearby observed floor spanning both XY axes and reports selection_distance_m and center_support_samples.
Release Z is the highest visible surface within R+5 mm of the fitted axis, plus R, tcp_offset and gap; this is an above-surface estimate.
Surface mode returns surface_point and a release_tcp also ≥30 mm above the column ceiling; rejects local height spread >4 mm, obstacles >60 mm above the sample or release rise >100 mm. It does not verify support or vacancy.
Both modes reject invalid/missing depth and local obstructions; recess mode also rejects unbounded flat/convex patches, poor fits, extrapolated centers and blocked release columns.
Geometric or valid-depth boundary rejection searches requested, half-size and 3-pixel patches at the selection and up to 24 neighbors within twice pixels; returns up to three checked candidates within R in world XY, with pixel/patch size and full diagnostics.
A continuous but obstructed surface selection also returns up to three separate surface_candidates: ≤48 probes within min(100 mm,4R), ≥R away, height within 4 mm, spacing ≥2R; each passes full surface/column checks. No support, vacancy or connectivity assertion.
Original plan_ok stays false with alternatives; missing selected depth, invalid arguments and discontinuities do not trigger surface search. Coordinates never execute automatically; concavity does not prove vacancy, identity, reachability, transit clearance or seating.
